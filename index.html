How to Use This Tool

▾
  1. Your notes auto-save as you type.
  2. Use "Add Item" for a new bullet point and "Add Subpoint" for an indented bullet.
  3. Drag the grip handle (or use Up/Down arrows) to reorder items and subpoints.
  4. Click "💾 Save snapshot" to save a named copy of your progress.
  5. Click "✨ Generate Meeting Notes" to create your report.
